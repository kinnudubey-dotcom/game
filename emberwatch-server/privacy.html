Emberwatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Emberwatch ("the game") uses. The game is published by {{DEVELOPER}} ("we", "us"). Questions: {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • A random player ID and secret key. The game creates them on your device the first time you open it. They are not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
  • Your wallet: your Embers balance, the skins you own, the skin you have equipped, and whether you bought the Starter Pack or Remove Ads. This is stored on our server so purchases cannot be lost or faked.
  • Purchase information: when you buy something through Google Play, the game sends Google's purchase token and product ID to our server. We use them only to check the purchase with Google and give you what you bought once. We do not see or store your card, UPI or other payment details. Google handles payment.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider (Render) may record your IP address and the time of requests for a short period for security and to keep the service running.
  • Game progress on your device: campaign progress, stars, best scores, settings and your chosen display name are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us unless online play is switched on.

Online play

If online multiplayer is available in your version, the game sends your display name, your equipped skin and your in-match movement and actions to our server and to the other players in your match. This data is used only to run that match and is not kept afterwards.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, exact or approximate location, or advertising ID. The game currently shows no ads and has no analytics or tracking tools. If we add ads in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it.

How we use data

Only to run the game: keep your Embers and purchases, verify and deliver purchases, prevent cheating and abuse, run online matches, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Google (Google Play and Google Play Billing) for purchases.
  • Render (our server hosting provider) because it stores and processes the data above for us.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Wallet data is kept while your player ID is active. You can delete it any time (see below). Purchase receipts may be kept after deletion, without any link to your player ID, for accounting, tax and fraud-prevention reasons. Server logs are kept for a short period set by our hosting provider.

Your choices and deleting your data

In the game, open Settings, then Delete my data. This erases your wallet record on our server and the game data on your device. Deleted purchases (including Embers, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads) cannot be restored or refunded by us. Refund requests for Google Play purchases follow Google Play's refund policy.

If you cannot open the game, email {{EMAIL}} from the device's player ID shown in your request, or describe your request and we will help. Because the data is not linked to your name or email, we may need the player ID to find it. If you uninstall the game without deleting your data, the record stays on our server but can no longer be reached from a new install.

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct and erase your personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to use them.

Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the game, email us and we will delete the data.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. The player key is stored on our server only as a hash. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For big changes, we will tell you in the game.
